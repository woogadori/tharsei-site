Privacy Policy

Tharsei: Hearts Up · Effective: To be set at launch (currently in closed testing) · If this English version differs from the Korean one, the Korean version applies.

Tharsei: Hearts Up ("Tharsei") is run by Kangmin Kwon (the "operator"). We care about your privacy and follow the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we use and protect it.

What we don't collect: your real name, phone number, profile photo, or precise location (coordinates or city). Your country comes from your phone's region setting; we don't use location permission.

2. How we use it

To run the service (releasing balloons, delivering letters, automatic translation), to check safety and handle reports, to show support resources when someone may be in crisis, and for statistics that can't identify anyone.

3. AI processing

Before a balloon or letter is posted, an AI checks it for safety and translates it. For this, only the text is sent to Anthropic. No email or other account information is sent. Under its contract, Anthropic does not use this text to train AI, and deletes it within 30 days. It may keep it longer only to investigate violations of its usage policy or when the law requires.

5. Keeping and deleting

  • When you delete your account, your balloons, letters, translations, stamp records and profile are deleted right away.
  • Only numbers that can't identify anyone remain, like total balloons, total letters and the amount given.
  • If an account is deleted after 5 strikes for breaking the community guidelines, we keep only a one-way hash of the Google account ID so it can't sign up again.
  • If an account that received the sign-up gift is deleted, we keep only a hash of the Google account ID so the gift isn't given twice. This hash can't tell us who you are.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep is kept for that period.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information at any time.

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account
  • Without the app: account deletion request page or tharsei.heartsup@gmail.com

7. Under 18

Tharsei is for people 18 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

8. Security

Encryption in transit, restricted database access (each person can reach only their own data), and secrets such as AI keys kept only on the server.

We'll announce changes in the app 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that are less favorable to you).
