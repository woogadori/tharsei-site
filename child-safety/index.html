Child Safety Standards

Tharsei: Hearts Up · by Kangmin Kwon

Tharsei lets adults send short stories (balloons) to strangers around the world and receive short letters of support back. Everything is text only. Tharsei has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

1. Adults only

Tharsei is only for people 18 and older. Every user confirms they are 18 or older when signing up. Users that Google identifies as minors can't download the app on Google Play. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

2. What is prohibited

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): creating, sharing, requesting or promoting it, including written descriptions.
  • Sexualizing minors in any way.
  • Trying to contact, befriend or get close to a minor, including grooming: building trust with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Asking anyone for contact details, social media accounts or a meeting. This is blocked for all users, so conversations can't move off Tharsei.

3. How we prevent it

  • Automatic safety check before posting. Every balloon and letter is checked by AI before anyone else can see it. Sexual content, contact details (including disguised ones) and plans to meet are rejected.
  • In-app reporting and blocking. Every balloon and every letter has a report button. Report reasons include “Sexual content” and “Someone may be in danger”. A reported post is hidden from everyone right away, and “Someone may be in danger” reports go to the top of the review queue. Users can block anyone in one tap.
  • No private threads, no photos. There are no ongoing one-to-one chats, and users can't send images, videos or files.

4. What happens when we find a violation

  • The content is removed immediately.
  • The account is permanently deleted, without the usual strike steps. The same Google account can't sign up again.
  • As required by law, we report it to the relevant authorities, such as the Korean National Police Agency or the U.S. National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

5. Child safety contact

To report a child safety concern, or for questions about these standards, email tharsei.heartsup@gmail.com. We review these reports first.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.
