Terms of Use

Tharsei: Hearts Up · Effective: To be set at launch (currently in closed testing) · If this English version differs from the Korean one, the Korean version applies.

1. Purpose

These terms set out how you may use Tharsei: Hearts Up (the "service"), provided by Kangmin Kwon (the "operator").

2. Words we use

  • Balloon: a story of up to 150 characters that you write. Other people see it anonymously.
  • Letter: a note of support or a reply of up to 150 characters, sent to a balloon or to a letter you received.
  • Stamp: an in-app pass. Releasing a balloon or sending a letter uses 1 stamp. Free stamps are refilled up to 3 every day at your local midnight (they don't pile up) and are used first; my stamps come from the sign-up gift, golden balloons and pieces.
  • Piece: you get one for each letter you receive. Five pieces become one stamp.

3. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. You sign up with a Google account, and by signing up you agree to these terms, the community guidelines and the Privacy Policy.

4. Stamps and pieces

  1. Stamps and pieces can be used only inside the app. They can't be exchanged for money or given to anyone else.
  2. Stamps you got for free (daily free stamps, sign-up gift, golden balloons and so on) can't be refunded.
  3. No stamp is used for a post that is rejected by the safety check or can't be sent. If one was already used, it is given back.
  4. When you delete your account, any stamps and pieces you have are gone.
  5. The operator may change how many stamps are given and how they are used, and will announce it in the app beforehand.

5. What you write

  1. You own what you write.
  2. You allow the operator to use your posts only as needed to run the service (showing them to others, translating, summarizing and safety checks). This permission ends when you delete the post or your account.
  3. Every post is checked by AI before it is posted. Automatic translations may differ from the original.

6. What's not allowed

Sharing or asking for contact details, social media or meetings (including disguised ways), swearing, hate or bullying, sexual content, ads or spam, pretending to be someone else, disrupting the service, and anything else against the community guidelines.

7. Reports and penalties

  1. A reported post is hidden right away and reviewed by the operator.
  2. If a violation is confirmed, you get one strike. At 5 strikes the account is deleted, and the same Google account can't sign up again.
  3. Serious violations may lead to deletion without strikes.
  4. If you disagree with a decision, write to tharsei.heartsup@gmail.com.

8. In a crisis

The service is not a substitute for professional counseling or medical care. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number or the support lines shown under "Need help?" in the app.

9. Giving promise

For every 1,000 letters of support sent in the service, the operator gives $1 to people in need and shares the record in the app. This is the operator's voluntary promise, not a fundraiser that collects users' money. The amount is reviewed every quarter, and any change is announced beforehand.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account anytime in Settings. Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone. To delete without the app, see the account deletion page.

11. Changes to the service

The operator may change or stop the service, and will announce important changes in the app beforehand.

12. Limits of responsibility

To the extent the law allows, the operator is not responsible for what users write to each other, the accuracy of translations, or outages that aren't the operator's fault, such as natural disasters.

13. Changes to these terms

We'll announce changes in the app 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that are less favorable to you).

14. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea. Disputes go to the court with jurisdiction under the Korean Civil Procedure Act.
